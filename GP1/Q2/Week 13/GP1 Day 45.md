The 38-minute block is a final sprint with circulation, and the gate check is simple: items bob and spin, the score chain reaches the screen, pickups award points on overlap, and the whole run is committed and pushed. The gate stays open into Day 46's first minutes for stragglers, so Thursday's chunk start does not stall.

## Structure

1. Do Now review (5 min): the public Points To Add variable answered cold; the preview math stays open until students run their own pickups.
2. Finish Videos 7, 8 & 9 block (38 min, circulation): final sprint with the gate, not lockstep teaching.
   - First 5 minutes: every student names their gap out loud to the room (the Do Now's blocker, said in one line); Nick's triage reads it.
   - Work time (25 min): catch-up lane for anyone short of V08 or V09; tuning and the two-pickup test for anyone through V09; nothing new gets started.
   - Commit gate at minute 40: every student commits and pushes with a message that names the chunk ("Pickup items 7-9"). Model the message on the board first: "Pickup items 7-9", not "update". Stragglers get the first 10 minutes of Day 46 to land the same commit, and their exit ticket names what still needs to push.
   - Gate check for each student, one glance: two pickups of different point values, the screen total climbing on overlap, the commit sitting in GitHub Desktop's history. Presence-based: the build being there earns it; the gate reads the room's position inside the chunk, not polish.
3. Exit ticket (5 min).

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement**: the room's gate is a scoreboard that answers you; the sprint framing is honest - the work is finishing, not attending.
- **Representation**: the gate check as three things the screen shows (two pickups, climbing total, pushed commit) rather than a rubric; the commit message modeled once on the board, then read back from GitHub Desktop.
- **Action & Expression**: the exit ticket's trace question lets a student prove the chain in numbers even if their build lagged; stragglers' tickets name tomorrow's first move instead of pretending the gate closed.
- **Accommodations**: the push failure this room already met - a commit that will not push because of an imported asset pack - gets the standing repair, ignore rule first then repo surgery for anything over 100 MB. A student with the chain built but never tested: the two-pickup test IS the check, so sit them at it first.
- **Student Voice**: the gap call in the first five minutes is the room grading itself; the blocker named in question 3 is what tomorrow's chunk-start lane reads first.
