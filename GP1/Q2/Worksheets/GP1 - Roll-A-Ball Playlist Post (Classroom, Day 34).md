# Roll-A-Ball Video Playlist

All Roll-A-Ball videos are in one playlist on EdPuzzle:

https://edpuzzle.com/playlist/692f66df455e17e25e872f54

How to use it:

- We watch ONE video per class day. Do not run ahead of the class unless the day sheet says so
- The questions are already inside each video - answer them as they come up
- Every video goes with work in YOUR Roll-A-Ball project, the one you accepted on Tuesday. Watching without doing it in the project doesn't count
- If you fall behind, checkpoints are your catch-up days, and the video list shows what order to work in

Bookmark the playlist. You'll come back to it every day.
