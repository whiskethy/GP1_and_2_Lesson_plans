# GP1 Q1 Day 18 — Transforms (Sub)

**Date:** Thu 9/24 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunk:** 3 (transforms/coordinate math)

Sub day. Students work self-paced through EdPuzzle Videos 04-06, starting with Video 04 (Transforms). The Transforms Worksheet is the last step, after Video 06.

## Learning Target

Use Unreal's coordinate system (X forward, Y sideways, Z up) to place objects at specific locations using transform values.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT G 406 (locate points in the coordinate plane), G 407 (translate points in the coordinate plane), WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Transforms (location/rotation/scale) are the foundation for placing objects and for understanding Blueprint variable types later. G 406 and G 407 (coordinate plane) apply directly here.

| # | Type | Question |

| 1 | Review | What are the three transform tools and their keyboard shortcuts? |

**Agenda for sub — one bucket, self-paced**

> Students start with the Do Now on the board (5 min). Then everyone works at their own pace through the same list, in order:
>
> 1. EdPuzzle Video 04 — Transforms, plus its tasks (place cubes at specific coordinates)
> 2. EdPuzzle Video 05 — Rotation, plus its tasks
> 3. EdPuzzle Video 06 — Scale, plus its tasks
> 4. Transforms Worksheet (copies on the teacher's desk by the keyboard), the last step, after Video 06
>
> Most students should be starting Video 04 today — students ahead may already be further down the list, and that's fine. Whatever a student doesn't finish picks up next class. No timed blocks.
>
> The sub does NOT need to teach content — the videos carry all instruction. Stuck for more than five minutes? Rewind the video section. Collect any completed worksheets at the end of the period.

**Exit ticket** (on paper)

1. What are the three parts of an object's Transform?
2. In Unreal, which direction is +X? Which direction is +Z?
3. A cube is 100 units wide. You place one cube at (0, 0, 50). Where would you place a second cube so it sits directly to the right of the first one?

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** The video includes coordinate placement challenges (place a cube next to, behind, and above another). These carry directly into independent practice.
- **Representation:** Axis diagram on the worksheet (red=X forward, green=Y sideways, blue=Z up). Video provides visual demonstration.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket on worksheet (written). Students work at their own pace through the EdPuzzle and practice tasks.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time on the 72-minute B-day. Printed axis color reference. Pair seating for students struggling with coordinate math.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which Starter Content objects to place in practice.
