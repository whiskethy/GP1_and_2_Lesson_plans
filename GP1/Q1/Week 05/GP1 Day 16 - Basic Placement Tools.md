# GP1 Q1 Day 16 — Finish Video 02: Levels and the Viewport

**Date:** Tue 9/22 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunks:** 1-2 (completion)

Video completion day. Students finish EdPuzzle Video 02, started on Day 15, and complete its tasks: folder structure, multi-view placement, and screenshots.

## Learning Target

Demonstrate viewport navigation and level management in Unreal by completing EdPuzzle Video 02 and its tasks: set up a project folder structure, place objects using multiple viewports, and take screenshots as evidence.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01, 3A-CS-02 · ACT WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Viewport fluency is prerequisite for every editor task left in Unit 3. Students who cannot navigate and place objects in multi-view will not be able to do the transform work starting tomorrow.

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** EdPuzzle pauses keep students active; the screenshot tasks give concrete evidence of progress. Students who finish early can place extra props of their choice in the multi-view challenge.
- **Representation:** Video provides visual and audio instruction; students can rewind and rewatch. Axis color diagram stays on the board.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket written or verbal. Students work at their own pace through the EdPuzzle.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time — students who need more than today to finish Video 02 can continue tomorrow alongside Video 03 (self-paced model). Pair seating for verbal check-ins.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which Starter Content props to place in the multi-view task.
