# GP1 Q1 Day 15 — EdPuzzle Videos 01 & 02

**Date:** Mon 9/21 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunks:** 1-2 (review)

Video day. Students work through EdPuzzle Video 01 and start Video 02, which cover editor panels, project creation, viewport navigation, levels, and multi-view placement. For most of the room this is first instruction in the editor; Video 02 continues on Day 16.

## Learning Target

Reinforce understanding of the Unreal Editor interface, project creation, viewport navigation, and level management through guided EdPuzzle viewing.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 (abstractions hide implementation details), 3A-CS-02 (compare levels of abstraction) · ACT WME 402 (technical vocabulary)

**Performance Task Connection:** Solidifying editor and viewport skills ensures students can work independently on subsequent transform and lighting days.

**Do Now** (retrieval, not a cold open)

| #   | Type    | Question                                                                     |
| --- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Review  | What does PIE stand for, and what do you use it for?                         |
| 2   | Review  | What's one way to look at your level from a different angle in the viewport? |
| 3   | Preview | What is the difference between perspective and orthographic views?           |

**Agenda**

0. Enter class → open Unreal. If you do not have a project yet, Video 01 walks through creating a Blank project with Starter Content.
1. Do Now review (5 min)
2. EdPuzzle Video 01 — Getting Started (approx. 18-25 min with pauses)
3. EdPuzzle Video 02 — Levels and the Viewport: start in the remaining time. Video 02's tasks (folder structure, multi-view placement, screenshots) continue on Day 16.
4. Wrap-up (3 min)

**Exit ticket** (on paper or verbal)

1. What are three ways to navigate the viewport?
2. Why does the Content Browser use folders instead of raw file paths?
3. What is the difference between perspective and orthographic view?

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** EdPuzzle pauses for questions, keeping students active. Tasks within the videos (create folders, place objects, take screenshots) give hands-on practice.
- **Representation:** Videos provide visual and audio instruction. Students can rewind and rewatch sections.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket written or verbal. Students work at their own pace through the EdPuzzle.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time. Students who finish early can explore the Content Browser or try multi-view placement. Pair seating for students who need verbal check-ins.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which Starter Content props to place in the Video 02 challenge task.
