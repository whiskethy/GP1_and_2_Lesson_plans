# GP1 Q1 Day 20 — Scale (Sub)

**Date:** Wed 9/30 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunk:** 3 (scale)

Sub day. Students watch EdPuzzle Video 06 (Scale) and practice scale in Unreal. The Transforms Worksheet is the last step of the day: students finish Videos 04, 05, and 06 first, then the worksheet consolidates all three.

## Learning Target

Use Unreal's Scale tool to resize objects, and explain the difference between uniform and non-uniform scaling.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT G 406, G 407, WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Transforms (location/rotation/scale) are the foundation for placing objects and for understanding Blueprint variable types later.

**Do Now** (retrieval, on worksheet)

| # | Type | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | What are the three rotation axes and their colors? |
| 2 | Review | What happens when you rotate an object by a negative value? |
| 3 | Preview | If you stretch a rubber band to twice its length, what happens to its width? Does it stay the same? |

**Agenda for sub**

> 1. Students enter and start the Do Now on paper (5 min)
> 2. EdPuzzle Video 06 — Scale (approx. 13-19 min with pauses). All instruction is in the video.
> 3. Independent practice: scale challenges in Unreal — uniform and non-uniform scaling, reset to default (15 min)
> 4. Transforms Worksheet — the last step. Students who watched Videos 04, 05, and 06 complete the full worksheet, Parts 1-5 (20 min)
> 5. Exit ticket on the worksheet, Part 5 challenge (5 min)
> 6. Students who finish early: move on to any outstanding video tasks (the Task List sheet on Google Classroom has the list), or add lights and extra objects to their scene
>
> The sub does NOT need to teach content — the video and the worksheet carry all instruction. Collect worksheets at the end of the period. If a student is stuck for more than five minutes, tell them to rewind the video section or check the worksheet's axis diagram.

**Exit ticket** (on paper)

1. An object is scaled to X=1, Y=1, Z=1. You change X to 3. What happened to the object?
2. What is the difference between uniform and non-uniform scaling?
3. What is the Scale tool keyboard shortcut, and what does the white center cube do when you drag it?

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** EdPuzzle questions keep students active. Stretching objects to weird proportions is inherently fun.
- **Representation:** Video provides visual demo. Printed axis color reference available.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket on paper (written). Students work at their own pace.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time. Pair seating for verbal check-ins.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which objects to scale in practice.
