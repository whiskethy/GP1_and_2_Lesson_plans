# GP1 Q1 Day 19 — Rotation (Sub)

**Date:** Tue 9/29 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunk:** 3 (rotation)

Sub day. Students continue the self-paced Videos 04-06 list, with most of the room on EdPuzzle Video 05 (Rotation) and practicing rotation in Unreal. The Transforms Worksheet is the last step, after Video 06.

## Learning Target

Use Unreal's Rotate tool to change object orientation, and explain how pitch, yaw, and roll correspond to X, Y, and Z rotation.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT G 406, G 407, WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Transforms (location/rotation/scale) are the foundation for placing objects and for understanding Blueprint variable types later.

**Do Now** (retrieval, on worksheet)

| # | Type | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | In Unreal, which axis is forward (+X) and which is up (+Z)? |
| 2 | Review | A cube is at (0, 0, 50). You want to place another cube directly behind it. What X value do you use? |
| 3 | Preview | If you rotate something 90 degrees, how much of a full circle is that? |

**Agenda for sub — one bucket, self-paced**

> Students start with the Do Now on the board (5 min). Then everyone works at their own pace through the same list, in order:
>
> 1. EdPuzzle Video 04 — Transforms, plus its tasks (place cubes at specific coordinates)
> 2. EdPuzzle Video 05 — Rotation, plus its tasks
> 3. EdPuzzle Video 06 — Scale, plus its tasks
> 4. Transforms Worksheet (copies on the teacher's desk by the keyboard), the last step, after Video 06
>
> Most students should be around Video 05 today. Students ahead may already be on the worksheet; students behind pick up wherever they left off — both are fine. No timed blocks.
>
> The sub does NOT need to teach content — the videos carry all instruction. Stuck for more than five minutes? Rewind the video section. Collect any completed worksheets at the end of the period.

**Exit ticket** (on paper)

1. What are the three rotation axes in Unreal, and which color matches each one?
2. A cube is rotated 90 degrees on the Z axis (yaw). Which direction does the top point now?
3. What happens when you rotate an object by a negative value?

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** EdPuzzle video pauses for embedded questions. Rotation challenges are hands-on.
- **Representation:** Video provides visual demo of each rotation ring. Axis color reference available.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket on paper (written). Students work at their own pace.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time. Students who finish early can try rotating objects to match screenshot challenges. Pair seating for verbal check-ins.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which objects to rotate in practice.
