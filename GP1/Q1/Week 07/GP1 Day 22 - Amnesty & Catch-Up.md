# GP1 - Amnesty & Catch-Up (Day 22)

Day 22: amnesty day. No new instruction. Students catch up on outstanding Unit 3 videos and the Transforms Practice packet, working from wherever they are in the series, before the unit moves forward on Day 23.

## Learning Target

Close the gap on your outstanding Unit 3 work: finish videos in the series, complete and submit the Transforms Practice packet, and get your Unreal project where it needs to be before the unit moves forward tomorrow.

## Do Now

| # | Type | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | Open your EdPuzzle dashboard. Where are you in the video series — which video are you on? Write it down. |
| 2 | Review | Did you turn in the Transforms Practice packet? If not, what's left on it? |
| 3 | Preview | Tomorrow we push forward in the unit. What's the ONE thing you need done today so you're not behind tomorrow? |

*(The Do Now doubles as the day's inventory: it shows exactly who owes what, without a spreadsheet pass.)*

## Exit Ticket

1. What did you finish today? (video number, packet, or both)
2. What's still owed, and when will you finish it? (tomorrow's class, lunch, home)
3. If you were done with everything: what did you work on instead?

## Agenda

1. Do Now review (5 min)
2. Amnesty time: finish videos, finish/submit the Transforms packet, catch up project work, teacher circulates and triages (35 min)
3. Exit ticket (5 min)
4. Wrap: preview tomorrow's push forward (3 min)

- Students done with everything may work on another class's work quietly. That's earned, not a free block — their dashboard has to show it.
- In parallel, unnumbered: Transforms packets are collected as students finish.

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** Amnesty with a visible finish line — the unit moves tomorrow, so today's catch-up has a real deadline, not an open-ended one
- **Representation:** EdPuzzle dashboard is the map; the Do Now makes each student read their own position. Video series stays available for rewatching
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket written; students choose their own catch-up order (videos vs packet)
- **Accommodations:** Extended time is inherent — the whole period is work time. Priority triage for students still before Video 03. Pair help allowed for students past the same video
- **Student Voice:** Students choose what to work on and what to do if caught up
