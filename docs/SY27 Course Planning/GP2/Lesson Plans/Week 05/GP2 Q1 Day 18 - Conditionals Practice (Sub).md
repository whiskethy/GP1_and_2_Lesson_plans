# GP2 Q1 Day 18 — Conditionals Practice (Sub)

**Date:** Fri 9/25 · **Unit:** 1 · **Chunk:** 13 (conditionals practice)

> **Sub coverage.** Nick is out. No new instruction. Two tasks, both already introduced: an auto-graded Google Form on conditionals (concept checks and code tracing), and continued work on the Variables Practice programs. The sub does not need content knowledge — the Form grades itself and the Classroom post has full directions.

## Learning Target

Write and trace conditional statements (if/else, else if) using comparison and logical operators, and finish the Variables Practice programs.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-AP-15, 3A-AP-17 · ACT WME 402, CLR 401

**Performance Task Connection:** Conditionals are required for game logic in every subsequent unit, and this is the only practice day on them before the mixed-practice consolidation. The Form checks the concepts; the repo work builds the program-writing fluency the Unit 1 Checkpoint assumes.

## Exit Ticket

1. Complete the Google Form — your score is your exit ticket.
2. Paste a link to your Variables Practice repo showing today's commits.

**Mastery Criteria:** Form score of 80%+ or a second attempt showing improvement. Repo shows at least one commit from today (rectangle-area or temperature-converter for students still finishing Variables Practice).

## Agenda for sub

> 1. Students enter, open Visual Studio and their repo, work the Do Now on paper (5 min)
> 2. Students open the Conditionals Practice Google Form from Google Classroom and complete it. The Form is auto-graded — students see their score immediately and may retry after reviewing the C++ Cheat Sheet (30 min)
> 3. Students continue Variables Practice in Visual Studio — rectangle-area, then temperature-converter — committing and pushing each program as it works (30 min)
> 4. Exit ticket: paste repo link in the Form's final question or on paper (5 min)
> 5. Students who finish early: take another pass at the Form to raise the score, or get ahead on the next Variables Practice program (2 min)
>
> The sub does NOT need to teach content. The Form grades itself. If a student is stuck for more than five minutes, the C++ Cheat Sheet (posted on Classroom) answers most syntax questions — point them there first. Nothing to collect — everything is digital.
