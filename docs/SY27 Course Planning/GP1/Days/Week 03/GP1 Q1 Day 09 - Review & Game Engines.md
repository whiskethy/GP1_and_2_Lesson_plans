# GP1 Q1 Day 09 — Review & Game Engines

**Date:** Tue 9/8 · **Unit:** 2 · **Chunk:** 9
**Planning Sheet:** [Week of 9/7](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eBWMHgWaEAkI7I6R8dwK7vmPgxCHK9VDh_oEGA-ouSw/edit)

## Learning Target

Explain what a game engine is and why game developers use one instead of building from scratch. Review Unit 1 concepts for the quiz.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT WME 402, IDT 403

**Performance Task Connection:** Students cannot compare game engines for the Unit 2 deliverable (engine comparison brief) without understanding what an engine does and why abstraction matters.

## Do Now

| # | Type    | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | What is a CPU? What does it do? |
| 2 | Review | Convert the hex value #2F to decimal. In RGBA, what does the A stand for? |
| 3 | Preview | Why would a developer choose to use an engine instead of building everything from scratch? |

## Exit Ticket

1. Name two things a game engine does for a developer. 2. Why would a developer choose to use an engine instead of building everything from scratch?

**Mastery Criteria:** Correctly identifies 2+ engine functions and explains why abstraction matters.

## Agenda

1. Do Now review (5 min)
2. Unit 1 review for quiz (8 min)
3. Game engine lesson - slides 1-12 (15 min)
4. Guided practice: "Why use an engine?" - pairs list 2+ engine functions from the lesson, then compare with a partner (8 min)
5. Independent practice: exit ticket draft - students write their answer to "Why would a developer choose to use an engine?" (7 min)
6. Wrap up + quiz reminder (5 min)

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** Choice in which engines to compare
- **Representation:** Visual pipeline diagram alongside verbal explanation
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket written or verbal
- **Accommodations:** Extended time; printed copies of slides
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which engines to compare and what matters most to them
