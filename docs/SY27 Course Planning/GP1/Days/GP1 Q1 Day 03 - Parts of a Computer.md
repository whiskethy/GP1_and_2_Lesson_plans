## Learning Target

Identify the major parts of a computer and explain what each does.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT WME 402

## Exit Ticket

1. Name three parts of a computer and what each does. 2. How does the CPU interact with RAM?

**Mastery Criteria:** Correctly identifies 3+ parts and explains their roles.

## Agenda

- **Representation:** Diagram on board + labeled visual
- **Action & Expression:** Verbal or written exit ticket
- **Accommodations:** Printed practice sheet
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which parts to explain in depth
