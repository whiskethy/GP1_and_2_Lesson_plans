# GP1 Q1 Day 17 — Transforms

**Date:** Wed 9/23 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunk:** 3
**Planning Sheet:** [Week of 9/21](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eBWMHgWaEAkI7I6R8dwK7vmPgxCHK9VDh_oEGA-ouSw/edit)

## Learning Target

Use Unreal's coordinate system (X forward, Y sideways, Z up) to place objects at specific locations using transform values.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT G 406 (locate points in the coordinate plane), G 407 (translate points in the coordinate plane), WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Coordinate placement is the foundation for the level layout practical (Chunk 9). Students who cannot place objects at specific coordinates will be unable to build intentional layouts.

**Do Now** (retrieval, not a cold open)

| # | Type | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | What are the three transform tools and their keyboard shortcuts? |
| 2 | Review | What are the default transform values for a new object? |
| 3 | Preview | On a graph, the point (3, -2) is in which quadrant? What would a third axis (Z) add? |

**Agenda**

0. Enter class → open your Unreal project. Place a cube at the origin (0,0,0).
1. Do Now review (5 min)
2. EdPuzzle Video 04 — Transforms (approx. 20-28 min with pauses)
3. Guided practice: place cubes at specific coordinates using the Details panel (10 min)
4. Independent: coordinate challenges from the video (place cubes next to, behind, and above the origin cube) (10 min)
5. Wrap-up + exit ticket (3 min)

**Exit ticket**

1. What are the three parts of an object's Transform?
2. In Unreal, which direction is +X? Which direction is +Z?
3. A cube is 100 units wide. You place one cube at (0, 0, 50). Where would you place a second cube so it sits directly to the right of the first one?

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Correctly identifies Location, Rotation, and Scale as the three transform parts, states that +X is forward and +Z is up, and calculates that the second cube goes at (0, 100, 50) or equivalent reasoning showing understanding of Y as the sideways axis.

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** The video includes coordinate placement challenges (place a cube next to, behind, and above another). These carry directly into guided practice.
- **Representation:** Axis diagram on the board (red=X forward, green=Y sideways, blue=Z up). Video provides visual demonstration.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket written or verbal. Students work at their own pace through the EdPuzzle and practice tasks.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time. Printed axis color reference. Pair seating for students struggling with coordinate math.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which Starter Content objects to place in practice.
