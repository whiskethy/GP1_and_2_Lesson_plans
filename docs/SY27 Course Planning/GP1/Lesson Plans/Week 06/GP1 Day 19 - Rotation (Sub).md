# GP1 Q1 Day 19 — Rotation (Sub)

**Date:** Tue 9/29 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunk:** 3 (rotation)

> **Sub coverage.** Nick is out. Students watch EdPuzzle Video 05 (Rotation) and practice rotation in Unreal. No worksheet today — the Transforms Worksheet comes after Video 06, as the last step of the sequence.

## Learning Target

Use Unreal's Rotate tool to change object orientation, and explain how pitch, yaw, and roll correspond to X, Y, and Z rotation.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT G 406, G 407, WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Rotation is required for the level layout practical (Chunk 9). Students need to orient objects correctly.

**Exit ticket** (on paper)

1. What are the three rotation axes in Unreal, and which color matches each one?
2. A cube is rotated 90 degrees on the Z axis (yaw). Which direction does the top point now?
3. What happens when you rotate an object by a negative value?

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Correctly matches at least two rotation axes to their colors (red=pitch/X, green=yaw/Y, blue=roll/Z), describes the result of a 90-degree yaw rotation, and explains that negative rotation goes in the opposite direction.
