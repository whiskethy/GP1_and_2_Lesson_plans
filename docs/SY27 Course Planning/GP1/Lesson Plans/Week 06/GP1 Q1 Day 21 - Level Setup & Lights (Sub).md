# GP1 Q1 Day 21 — Level Setup & Transforms Consolidation (Sub)

**Date:** Thu 10/1 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunks:** 5 (level setup/lights) + 3 (consolidation)

> **Sub coverage.** Nick is out. Students watch EdPuzzle Video 07 (Level Setup) and practice lighting in Unreal. The Transforms Worksheet was completed Wednesday — students who still owe parts of it finish today. Nick is back Monday.

## Learning Target

Apply location, rotation, and scale transforms to place objects at specific positions, orientations, and sizes in Unreal. Identify and place light types in Unreal.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT G 406, G 407, WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Consolidating transform skills is required for the level layout practical (Chunk 9). Lighting is also required for the practical.

**Agenda for sub**

> 2. EdPuzzle Video 07 — Level Setup (approx. 20-28 min with pauses). All instruction is in the video.
> 3. Independent practice: add lights to the scene from the video — Directional, Sky, Point, Spot — and place objects to match the video's setup (25 min)

> 6. Students who finish early: move on to any outstanding video tasks (the Task List sheet on Google Classroom has the list), or keep polishing their lit scene. Nick is back Monday.

> The sub does NOT need to teach content — the video carries all instruction. Collect exit tickets (and any finished worksheets) at the end of the period. If a student is stuck for more than five minutes, tell them to rewind the video section.

**Exit ticket** (on paper)

1. Place a cube at X=300, Y=-200, Z=50. What are its coordinates in the Details panel?
2. Rotate a cylinder 90 degrees on the Z axis (yaw). Which direction does the top point now?
3. Scale a sphere to X=2, Y=2, Z=2. What shape does it become?

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Coordinates match (300, -200, 50), top points sideways (or equivalent description), and sphere remains a sphere (uniform scale does not change shape).

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** EdPuzzle video pauses for embedded questions, keeping students active. The worksheet gives concrete coordinate, rotation, and scale challenges to complete in Unreal.
- **Representation:** Video provides visual demo for lighting. Worksheet has written instructions, coordinate references, and axis diagrams.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket on the worksheet (written). Students work at their own pace through the video and worksheet.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time. Students who finish the worksheet early can explore adding lights to their scene. Pair seating for verbal check-ins.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which objects to place and transform in the worksheet challenges.
