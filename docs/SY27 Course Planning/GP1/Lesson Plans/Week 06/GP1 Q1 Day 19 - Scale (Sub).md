**Exit ticket** (on paper)

1. An object is scaled to X=1, Y=1, Z=1. You change X to 3. What happened to the object?
2. What is the difference between uniform and non-uniform scaling?
3. What is the Scale tool keyboard shortcut, and what does the white center cube do when you drag it?

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Explains that X=3 makes the object three times as wide along X, states that uniform scaling changes all axes equally while non-uniform changes them independently, and identifies the R key for Scale tool with the white cube scaling uniformly.
