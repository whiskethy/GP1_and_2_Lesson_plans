# GP1 Q1 Day 02 — What a Computer Is

**Date:** Tue 8/25 · **Unit:** 1 · **Chunk:** 2

## Learning Target

Explain what a computer is and why it uses binary.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT WME 402

## Do Now

| # | Type    | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | What is one class norm we agreed on yesterday? |
| 2 | Review | (see Review 1 above) |

## Exit Ticket

1. Convert the binary number 1011 to decimal. 2. Why do computers use binary instead of decimal?

**Mastery Criteria:** Correctly converts binary to decimal and explains why computers use binary.

## Agenda

1. Do Now review (5 min)
2. What is a computer? lesson (20 min)
3. Binary intro + guided practice (18 min)
4. Wrap up (5 min)

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** Binary puzzles at different difficulty levels
- **Representation:** Visual binary-to-decimal conversion on board
- **Action & Expression:** Written or verbal exit ticket
- **Accommodations:** Printed reference sheet for binary place values
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which binary problems to attempt
