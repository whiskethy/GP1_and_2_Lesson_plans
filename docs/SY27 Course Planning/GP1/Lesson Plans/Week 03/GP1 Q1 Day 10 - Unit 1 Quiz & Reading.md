# GP1 Q1 Day 10 — Unit 1 Quiz & Reading

**Date:** Wed 9/9 · **Unit:** 2 · **Chunk:** 9/reading

## Learning Target

Demonstrate mastery of Unit 1 concepts (binary, hex, RGB, computer parts).

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-DA-09, 3A-CS-01 · ACT WME 402, N 302, IDT 403

**Performance Task Connection:** The Unit 1 quiz measures mastery of concepts (binary, hex, data representation) that are prerequisite for all subsequent units. Without these foundations, students cannot troubleshoot in the Unreal editor or debug Blueprint variable types.
