# GP1 Q1 Day 06 — Hexadecimal & Color Codes

**Date:** Tue 9/1 · **Unit:** 1 · **Chunk:** 6

## Learning Target

Convert between binary, decimal, and hexadecimal. Apply hex codes to represent colors.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT WME 402, N 302

## Do Now

| # | Type    | Question |
|---|---------|----------|

| 2 | Review | (see Review 1 above) |

## Exit Ticket

1. Convert 255 from decimal to hex. 2. In RGBA, what does the A stand for and what does it control? 3. What color is #FF0000?

**Mastery Criteria:** Correctly converts to hex and explains RGBA.

## Agenda

1. Do Now review (5 min)
2. Hex numbers lesson (15 min)
3. RGBA and hex color codes lesson (10 min)
4. Hex + RGB practice (18 min)
5. Wrap up (5 min)
