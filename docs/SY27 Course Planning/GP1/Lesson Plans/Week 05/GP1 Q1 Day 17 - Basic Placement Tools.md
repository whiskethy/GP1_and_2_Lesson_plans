# GP1 Q1 Day 17 — Basic Placement Tools

**Date:** Wed 9/23 · **Unit:** 3 · **Chunk:** 3

## Learning Target

Identify the three transform tools (Move, Rotate, Scale) and their keyboard shortcuts, and use them to place, rotate, and scale objects in Unreal.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-CS-01 · ACT G 406, G 407, WME 402

**Performance Task Connection:** Understanding all three transform tools is prerequisite for the coordinate math in Video 04 (Day 18) and for every subsequent build task in the level layout practical (Chunk 9).

**Do Now** (retrieval, not a cold open)

| # | Type | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | Where does Unreal keep all of your project's assets inside the editor? |
| 2 | Review | What's one way to move the camera around your level in the viewport? |
| 3 | Preview | If you wanted to turn a chair to face a table, what would you need to change — its location, its rotation, or its scale? |

**Agenda**

1. Do Now review (5 min)
2. EdPuzzle Video 03 — Basic Placement Tools (approx. 22-30 min with pauses)
3. Guided practice: try all three transform tools on objects in your scene (10 min)
4. Independent: place, rotate, and scale objects to match a target arrangement (10 min)
5. Wrap-up + exit ticket (3 min)

- **In parallel (not a timed block):** Unit 1 quiz retake. Students retaking the binary/hex quiz work on it during the Video 03 block instead of starting the video, then start Video 03 after submitting and catch up at their own pace.
- **Also in parallel:** any student who hasn't submitted the Video 02 tasks (folder structure, multi-view placement, screenshots) finishes and submits those today alongside Video 03.

**~80% checkpoint:** By guided practice, most of the room has tried Move, Rotate, and Scale at least once. By the exit ticket, every student has either completed the video or has a hand up with a specific blocker.

**Exit ticket**

1. What are the three transform tools and their keyboard shortcuts?
2. What are the default transform values for a new object? (Location: ___, Rotation: ___, Scale: ___)
3. What does the Scale tool's white center cube do compared to dragging a colored axis?

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Correctly names all three tools (Move/W, Rotate/E, Scale/R), identifies default values as Location 0,0,0 / Rotation 0,0,0 / Scale 1,1,1, and explains that the white center cube scales uniformly while a colored axis stretches only that direction.

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** The video includes a game engine systems overview and hands-on placement challenges. Students who finish early can try placing chairs around a table.
- **Representation:** Video provides visual demo. Axis color diagram (red=X, green=Y, blue=Z) stays on the board.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket written or verbal. Students work at their own pace through the EdPuzzle.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time. Printed step list for transform tools (W=Move, E=Rotate, R=Scale). Pair seating for verbal check-ins. Quiz retakers get the retake out of the way first, then catch up on Video 03 at their own pace.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which Starter Content objects to transform in practice.
