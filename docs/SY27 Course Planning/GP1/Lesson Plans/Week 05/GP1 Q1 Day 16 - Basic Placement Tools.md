   - **Binary/hex retake quiz tomorrow.** Anyone who wants to retake the Unit 1 quiz does it in class tomorrow, while the rest of the class works on Video 03.

**~80% checkpoint:** By the exit ticket, every student has either completed Video 02 with screenshots taken or has a hand up with a specific blocker. Nobody sits stuck silently for more than five minutes.

**Exit ticket** (Day 15's questions, re-issued — they assess Video 02 content, which completes today)

1. What are three ways to navigate the viewport?
2. Why does the Content Browser use folders instead of raw file paths?
3. What is the difference between perspective and orthographic view?

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Correctly identifies at least two navigation modes (orbit, fly, pan, zoom, focus), explains that the Content Browser is an abstraction hiding file system complexity, and states that perspective shows depth while orthographic shows a flat view from one angle.

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** EdPuzzle pauses keep students active; the screenshot tasks give concrete evidence of progress. Students who finish early can place extra props of their choice in the multi-view challenge.
- **Representation:** Video provides visual and audio instruction; students can rewind and rewatch. Axis color diagram stays on the board.
- **Action & Expression:** Exit ticket written or verbal. Students work at their own pace through the EdPuzzle.
- **Accommodations:** Extended time — students who need more than today to finish Video 02 can continue tomorrow alongside Video 03 (self-paced model). Pair seating for verbal check-ins.
- **Student Voice:** Students choose which Starter Content props to place in the multi-view task.
