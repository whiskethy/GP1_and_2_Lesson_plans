## Exit Ticket

1. What did you finish today? (video number, packet, or both)
2. What's still owed, and when will you finish it? (tomorrow's class, lunch, home)
3. If you were done with everything: what did you work on instead?

**Mastery Criteria:** Every student leaves with a named next step. "Nothing owed" is only allowed if the EdPuzzle dashboard and the packet pile agree. The exit ticket is the day's evidence — Nick grades nothing today but knows exactly where everyone stands.
