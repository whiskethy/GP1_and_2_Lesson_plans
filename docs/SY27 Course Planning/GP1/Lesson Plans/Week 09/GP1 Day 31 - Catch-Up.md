## Exit ticket

1. Which videos are finished, and which will you walk in with incomplete?
2. What will your recording show first, and why that thing?
3. Anything broken right now? Name it and the node or system you suspect.

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Q1: position named honestly (video numbers) - the recording standard is what exists, not what is done. Q2: a specific opening beat tied to a checklist line. Q3: a real suspicion (Cast target, collision preset, wiring chain) or "nothing is broken" said after actually pressing play.
