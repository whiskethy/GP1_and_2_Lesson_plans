## Do Now

| # | Type | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | Open your ship game and press play. Does the ship move with space, fly forward, and disappear the ring you hit? Name what works and what doesn't. |
| 2 | Review | What does your score display show when you hit a ring right now - the number ticking up, or nothing? If nothing, where does the chain break? |
| 3 | Preview | In one sentence: what is your game going to SHOW the viewer - what's the one thing you built that you're proudest of? |

## Submission shape (Nick's standing Devlog format)

- OBS screen recording (instructional video/doc posted by Nick)
- Recording shows the checklist; presence of each item earns its points
- Strategic items double as mastery evidence
- No narration (narration starts Q2)
- 3-4 reflection questions answered in the assignment

## Do Now doubles as the pre-flight check

Q1 and Q2 together ARE the pre-flight: a student who answers Q1 or Q2 with "doesn't work" has today's class time to fix it BEFORE recording, not after. The checkpoint records what actually works by end of day.

## Exit ticket

1. Which checklist item were you proudest to show, and why? (This is the "what did you customize" line asked as reflection.)
2. In the recording, what is your game's STORY - what's the player trying to do, and how does the score tell them they did it?
3. What would you add or fix next if the quarter didn't end (one specific item)?

**Exit ticket mastery criteria:** Q1: names a specific item (a mechanic, a build detail, a visual choice) with a real reason. Q2: goal stated as fly-through-the-rings, score ticking as the progress signal, either phrased by the student or echoed from the checklist - real wording counts more than formal language. Q3: an actual item (a mechanic, a game-feel change, a UI idea) - "nothing" or "I don't know" doesn't count, and "add more rings" only counts if they say what would be different.

## Agenda

1. Do Now review + pre-flight (5 min)
2. Fix what's broken from the pre-flight answers - this is the buffer for anyone the refactor broke yesterday (10 min)
3. OBS recording against the checklist (20-25 min, circulating)
4. Submit recording + reflection questions on Classroom (5-8 min, runs into the bell if needed)

**~80% checkpoint (mid-build, replaces the standard one):** by 20 minutes in, every student has either a recording in progress or, if stuck, is paired with a working neighbor. Nobody is recording an empty checklist - if the pre-flight said "broken," they're in item 2 first.

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** the devlog is their own game being shown and defended, not a quiz. The checklist credits what they chose (customizations) alongside what the unit required.
- **Representation:** date-based evidence, not pacing-based: students submit what exists today. OBS instructions are Nick's standing visual guide.
- **Action & Expression:** the medium IS expression - a recording, not paragraphs. Reflection questions give room for written or typed voices.
- **Accommodations:** extended time inherent (48 min is all today's). Students without OBS experience pair with a peer mid-recording. The fix-buffer in agenda item 2 absorbs anyone yesterday's refactor broke.
- **Student Voice:** reflection Q3 is deliberately open; the "show your customizations" checklist line is always student-chosen.

## Brief checklist source (what the recording needs to show, per Videos 11-16)

Every item earns its points on presence. Strategic lines double as mastery evidence. Items cite the video that teaches them, following the standing Devlog format.

- Show your ship imported and named appropriately (Video 11)
- Show the ship flying forward when you press play (Video 13) - double-evidence: proves Game Mode setup (11), Add Force (13), and movement in one line
- Show the spacebar impulse (bounce) working (Video 12)
- Show a ring disappearing when the ship overlaps it (Video 14) - double-evidence: overlap event + Cast + Destroy Actor
- Show the score ticking up when you score (Video 15) - double-evidence: Player Controller Score variable + Widget display + the wiring chain
- Show your Event Graph(s), scrolling slowly (Videos 12-16) - the refactored organization with functions visible (Video 16)
- Show your customizations (standing line: what you chose - label text, font, colors, collider sizing, obstacle layout)
