**GP1: Intro to Unreal Checkpoint 01**

Today's checkpoint shows where you are as of today. It is not a race. Submit evidence for every video you have actually finished by today. If you are not through a video, you do not submit evidence for it. You can still get full credit for what you have done.

**What you submit:**

Evidence screenshots, one per video you have finished, attached to this assignment:

| Video | What the screenshot shows |
|---|---|
| 07 - Level Setup | Your level with its lighting and sky set up the way you arranged it |
| 08 - Importing & Basic Materials | Your Content Browser showing the Meshes and Materials folders, plus the ring with a material applied in the viewport |
| 09 - Ring Blueprint | Your spinning ring in the viewport, or the BP_Ring Event Graph showing the BeginPlay print and the Tick spin |
| 10 - Blueprint Visual Programming | Your Event Graph with all three additions: a spin value you picked yourself, a second Print String off BeginPlay with your own text, and a Get Actor Rotation path printing as a string. Graph compiled with zero errors. |

**If you finished Video 10 today**, also type one sentence in the assignment: why did you pick the spin value you picked?

**Stuck today?** If your compile shows errors, read the first error in the Compiler Results panel out loud to yourself. It usually names the line. If you are behind on videos, use today's class time to catch up first, then submit what you have before the bell.

**When you finish early:** polish the ring (material, spin axis, extra prints) or help a neighbor at the same step. Per standing rule, helping is allowed for people already past that step.

## Teacher notes (do not post)

**Mastery criteria, by tier:** each video's evidence is graded binary (present and consistent, or not): Video 07 shows a deliberate lighting/sky setup; Video 08 shows folders plus an applied material; Video 09 shows a working BP_Ring Blueprint with BeginPlay print and Tick spin; Video 10 shows the three modifications compiled plus the one-sentence spin-value why. Full credit for every video the student submits evidence for; missing videos are not penalized today, they are just visible.

**Why tiers and not all four:** the checkpoint is date-based (students submit what they have) so the deadline holds for every student and no one's grade depends on being at Video 10. The four-item Video 10 build stays the stretch tier for students who got there.

**Anticipated misconception and its branch:** students who finished Video 10 will wire the rotation print onto Event Tick along with the spin, then flood the screen with rotation spam every frame. If you see it, that is the moment exit ticket Q2 exists for: it asks whether the rotation print should be a Tick job, so you can let a few students hit the spam live if it happens and ask the question out loud.

**Timing fallback:** if the room runs slow and the checkpoint starts with less than 15 minutes left, a student who finished Video 10 can submit the graph screenshot for items 1 and 2 only (custom spin + second print): the rotation print is the only piece that needs the video's last section. Tiers below that unchanged.
