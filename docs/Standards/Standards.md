# Standards

Reference copies of the standards frameworks relevant to Game Programming 1 & 2, cleaned up from raw PDF extracts and restructured so individual standards can be linked directly (e.g. `[[CSTA Standards#^3A-AP-13]]`) when writing lesson plans.

## Notes in this folder
- [[CSTA Standards]] — CSTA K-12 Computer Science Standards (2017), 120 standards across Levels 1A-3B.
- [[ISBE Computer Science Standards]] — Illinois Computer Science Standards (2022), 151 standards across Grades K-2 to 11-12.
- [[ISTE Standards]] — ISTE Standards (2024, v4.01), 26 standards / 100 indicators across Students, Educators, Education Leaders, and Coaches.
- [[CCRS-ReadingStandards]] - the [ACT](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/college-and-career-readiness/standards.html) College and Career Readiness Standards, which describe the essential skills students need across English, math, reading, science, and writing
- [[CCRS-MathematicsStandards]] - the [ACT](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/college-and-career-readiness/standards.html) College and Career Readiness Standards, which describe the essential skills students need across English, math, reading, science, and writing
