# GP2 Day 23 - Unit 1 Checkpoint Quiz & Amnesty

Day 23: the conditionals quiz is the Unit 1 Checkpoint. After the quiz, amnesty time for missing Unit 1 work. Roll-A-Ball in C++ starts Wednesday.

## Learning Target

Demonstrate mastery of Unit 1 conditionals on a written checkpoint quiz, then use remaining class time to close out any missing Unit 1 work.

**Standards:** CSTA 3A-AP-15, 3A-AP-17 · ACT WME 402, CLR 401, S 404

**Performance Task Connection:** The quiz is the Unit 1 gate. Conditionals are the logic Roll-A-Ball's player input needs, so Wednesday's start assumes this checkpoint is behind them. Amnesty closes out the owed work the checkpoint doesn't measure (Variables Practice, Conditionals Practice programs).

## Do Now

| # | Type | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Review | What does `else` do in a conditional chain, and when does its code run? |

## Agenda

1. Do Now (5 min)
2. Quiz logistics: Form link, write questions go on blank paper and get turned in, no talking during the quiz (3 min)
3. Unit 1 Checkpoint Quiz (~20 min)
4. Check your score. Below 80%: review your misses against the C++ Cheat Sheet, then retry. 80% or above: move to amnesty (15 min)
5. Amnesty: Variables Practice programs, Conditionals Practice, anything still owed from Unit 1. The teacher circulates (runs inside item 4's window for retry students)
6. Exit ticket + preview: Roll-A-Ball in C++ starts Wednesday (5 min)

## Exit Ticket

1. Which quiz question was hardest for you, and what concept was it testing?
2. What did you finish during amnesty today?
3. What's still owed, and when will you finish it?

## Differentiation & Accommodations

- **Engagement:** Instant score on the Form, retry built in, and amnesty with a real finish line. Unit 1 closes today.
- **Representation:** Quiz questions show code blocks, not prose descriptions. C++ Cheat Sheet for post-quiz review of misses.
- **Action & Expression:** Write questions answered on paper, the Form handles the multiple choice. Students choose their own amnesty order.
- **Accommodations:** ~20 minutes of quiz for 23 available, so extended time is inherent. Retry-after-review is the built-in second shot for students below 80%, no retake scheduling needed.
- **Student Voice:** Students pick what amnesty time goes to.
